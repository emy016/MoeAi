HOW IT WORKS

Three layers a chatbot doesn't have.

01

Your curriculum

Before answering, MoeAI searches your actual lectures and anything you've uploaded, then cites the part it used. No material? It says so instead of inventing a syllabus.

Live · 60 lectures indexed
02

Your language

Egyptian Arabic, Franco-Arabic or English, decided per message and enforced, so technical terms stay readable without flipping the whole answer to English.

Live · 19 regression tests
03

You

Every quiz question you miss becomes a note. Next week it's brought back up — as continuity, not as “according to my records”. You can read and delete all of it.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

What it won't do.

It won't invent your syllabus

With no material retrieved, it tells you it's answering from general knowledge.

It won't do your homework

It teaches the method. Submitting its output as yours is your call, and your risk.

It won't read other students

Row-level security on every table. Your library and notes are yours.

It won't pretend to be right

It admits mistakes and says when it's unsure. MoeAI can still be wrong — check what matters.

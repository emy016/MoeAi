HOW IT WORKS

Three layers a chatbot doesn't have.

01

Your curriculum

Before answering, MoeAI searches your actual lectures and anything you've uploaded, then cites the part it used. No material? It says so instead of inventing a syllabus.

Live · Logic Design, Differential Equations, Linear Algebra
02

Your language

Egyptian Arabic, Franco-Arabic or English, decided per message and enforced, so technical terms stay readable without flipping the whole answer to English.

Live · 19 regression tests
03

You

MoeAI remembers each student: your name, how you like things explained, your exam dates, the mistake you keep making. Some things it keeps in mind always, others only when that topic comes up. Open Memory in the app to read, pin, edit or delete every one of them, switch on skills, or tell it how to work with you.

Live · memory, skills, custom instructions

FOR UNIVERSITIES

Upload a course once. Every student gets a tutor that knows it.

Professors and TAs manage their course's MoeAI from the Tutor page: upload lectures, sheets and past exams; MoeAI reads them, builds a map of the course, the formulas and the mistakes students make, and flags what is missing. Anything MoeAI writes on its own waits for staff approval, and staff can ask it exactly what a student would to see how it answers. Students sign in through the university and find their courses already there.

Organizations get an admin dashboard: members, roles, invitations tied to a verified email, suspensions, and the plan. Every permission is enforced by the database, not the browser.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

What it won't do.

It won't invent your syllabus

With no material retrieved, it tells you it's answering from general knowledge.

It won't do your homework

It teaches the method. Submitting its output as yours is your call, and your risk.

It won't read other students

Row-level security on every table. Your library and notes are yours.

It won't hold your data hostage

Download everything it stores about you, or delete your account, from settings.

It won't pretend to be right

It admits mistakes and says when it's unsure. MoeAI can still be wrong — check what matters.

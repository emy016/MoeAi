For universities

Tutor mode, in the same app.

Course staff sign in with their staff ID and the app opens in tutor mode. They upload a course once and every enrolled student gets a MoeAI that knows it. Nothing MoeAI writes on its own reaches students before staff approve it.

Managers see who is active and how MoeAI is doing. They see that a student asked something, never what they asked.

What it won't do.

It won't invent your syllabus

When nothing in your course matches, it says it is answering from general knowledge.

It won't do your homework

It teaches the method. Handing in its output as yours is your call and your risk.

It won't read other students

Row-level security on every table. Your library and notes are yours.

It won't show your chats to staff

Lecturers see that you asked, never what you asked.

It won't hold your data hostage

Download everything it stores about you, or delete your account, from Settings.

It won't pretend to be right

It admits mistakes and says when it is unsure. Check what matters against your notes.

EduMoeAI

Made by a student who wanted a better way to learn.

EduMoe is an independent learning system for Computer Science students at FUE—built from real study sessions, shared explanations and the belief that good learning tools should be easier to find.

Explore the curriculumMeet MoeAI

Understanding should feel connected.

Lectures, practice, tools, deadlines and AI help usually live in separate places. EduMoe brings them into one context so a student can move from confusion to explanation, experimentation and recall without rebuilding a study setup every time.

The project began with explanations shared through the CS Epic Save Telegram community. It is still being built in public by students who are taking the same courses.
